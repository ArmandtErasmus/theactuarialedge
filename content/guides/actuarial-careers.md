## How to use this guide

This guide brings together the main graduate programmes, vacation work and insight opportunities for actuarial and quantitative students in South Africa, plus a directory of employers that regularly hire actuarial talent.

Recruitment calendars, eligibility rules and links change every year. The timings below are indicative, based on recent cycles. Always confirm the current dates and requirements on the employer's own careers page before you plan around them. Many programmes recruit a year or more before the start date, so start looking early in your penultimate or final year.

## Graduate and vacation programmes

### Nedbank Quant Graduate Programme

- **What it is:** a two-year rotational programme for actuarial and quantitative graduates across analytics, risk and quant roles.
- **Who can apply:** students registered for a postgraduate qualification in actuarial science, mathematics, statistics, financial mathematics, engineering or a related quantitative field.
- **Typical timing:** applications have opened around May to June for the following year's intake.
- **More information:** [Nedbank Explore Quants](https://jobs.nedbank.co.za/content/Explore-Quants/?locale=en_GB)

### Nedbank Quants Exploration Programme

- **What it is:** an insight programme introducing students to Nedbank's quant roles, culture and values.
- **Who can apply:** final-year undergraduate or postgraduate students in actuarial science or a related quantitative degree.
- **Typical timing:** applications have opened around August.
- **More information:** [Nedbank Explore Quants](https://jobs.nedbank.co.za/content/Explore-Quants/?locale=en_GB)

### PwC Actuarial, Risk and Quants (ARQ) Vacation Work

- **What it is:** consulting experience for actuarial students, with workshops and a guided research project.
- **Who can apply:** final-year actuarial students.
- **Typical timing:** information has been released around June, with deadlines typically in October.
- **More information:** [PwC ARQ students](https://www.pwc.co.za/en/careers/students/actuarial-risk-quants.html)

### PwC ARQ Graduate Programme

- **What it is:** a three-year programme with rotations across actuarial and quantitative finance work.
- **Who can apply:** Honours or final-year actuarial students.
- **Typical timing:** applications have opened around April.
- **More information:** [PwC ARQ students](https://www.pwc.co.za/en/careers/students/actuarial-risk-quants.html)

### Deloitte Actuarial and Insurance Solutions (AIS) Graduate Programme

- **What it is:** a structured actuarial consulting programme focused on insurance solutions and analytics.
- **Who can apply:** BSc or BBusSc graduates in actuarial science or financial mathematics (postgraduate study preferred). Recent cycles have asked for a minimum of five ASSA exemptions.
- **Typical timing:** applications have opened around March to April, with deadlines in May.
- **More information:** [Deloitte actuarial graduate programme](https://www.deloitte.com/za/en/careers/explore-your-fit/students/actuarial-graduate-programme.html)

### EY Actuarial and Quants Graduate Programme

- **What it is:** a graduate programme applying advanced quantitative techniques in financial services, risk and valuation.
- **Who can apply:** Honours or higher in actuarial science, mathematics, statistics, data science or another relevant quantitative degree.
- **Typical timing:** applications usually open between March and May each year.
- **More information:** [EY graduate programmes South Africa](https://www.ey.com/en_za/careers/graduate-programmes-south-africa)

### KPMG Actuarial Graduate Programme

- **What it is:** a three-year pathway towards qualifying as an actuary, with strong study support and exposure to life insurance, general insurance and pensions.
- **Who can apply:** BSc, BBusSc or BCom graduates in actuarial science or a related quantitative degree.
- **Typical timing:** applications have opened around May for the following year's intake.
- **More information:** [KPMG actuarial careers](https://kpmg.com/za/en/home/careers/actuarial.html)

### FirstRand Quant and Data Analytics Graduate Programme

- **What it is:** a two-year development programme with exposure to several business areas.
- **Who can apply:** final-year, Honours or Masters students in actuarial science, mathematical statistics, financial risk, engineering or a related quantitative degree.
- **Typical timing:** applications have opened around February to March for the following year's intake.
- **More information:** [FirstRand Quant programme](https://www.start.firstrand.co.za/Home/Quant)

### Actuaries in the Making (AIM) Virtual Vacation Work

- **What it is:** a virtual programme introducing actuarial students to real industry tasks and professional practice.
- **Who can apply:** final-year actuarial students.
- **Typical timing:** applications have opened around September to October for a January intake.
- **More information:** [Actuaries in the Making on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/actuaries-in-the-making/)

## Making your application count

- **Track deadlines.** Keep a simple calendar of opening and closing dates. Several of the largest programmes close months before the academic year ends.
- **Tailor your CV.** Match each application to the role, using the graduate CV template and the ATS keyword guide on this site.
- **Show practical skills.** A portfolio project, such as a reserving engine or a pricing model, gives you concrete material for the CV and the interview.
- **Use vacation work and insight programmes.** They are often the most direct route into a graduate offer, and they help you decide between consulting, insurance and banking.
